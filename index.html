How to Play — Texas Hold’em

Each hand you and five computer opponents get two private hole cards. Five community cards are dealt face up in the middle across four rounds of betting: preflop, flop (3 cards), turn (1 card), and river (1 card). Make the best five-card hand from your two hole cards and the five community cards.

Betting

  • Fold — give up the hand.
  • Check — pass, only when no bet faces you.
  • Call — match the current bet.
  • Bet / Raise — use the slider or presets, then confirm.
  • All-in — push your entire stack.

Two players (small & big blind) post forced bets before cards are dealt. If a player goes all-in for less than others have staked, extra chips form a side pot that only the larger stacks compete for.
